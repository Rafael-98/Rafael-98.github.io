Symmetry-protected topological phases (SPTs) with short-range entanglement include many states central to topological condensed matter physics, and their extension to gapless SPTs is key to understanding their consequences. We identify a fundamental connection between gapless SPTs and multiplicative topological phases, showing that multiplicative phases are an intuitive and general route to concrete models of gapless SPTs. In particular, combining canonical topological insulator and semimetal models with critical gapless models in symmetry-protected tensor-product constructions naturally realizes higher-dimensional, stable and intrinsic gapless SPTs, opening avenues for a broader investigation of topology through short-range entanglement.
