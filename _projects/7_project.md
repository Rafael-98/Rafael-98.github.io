Higher-form symmetries generalize conventional global symmetries and act on lower-dimensional submanifolds of a quantum system. Abelian topological phases are organized by 1-form symmetries that form a group, but non-Abelian topological phases based on finite groups require 1-form symmetry operators governed by non-invertible fusion algebras. We make this precise in quantum double lattice models $$\mathcal D(G)$$: we construct the electric, magnetic and dyonic 1-form operators directly at the lattice fixed point, show that they form a complete nonlocal diagnostic algebra for the topological Hilbert space, and use them to determine the cylinder and torus ground-state subspaces for any finite $$G$$. This establishes non-Abelian quantum double ground states as a concrete microscopic realization of spontaneous non-invertible 1-form symmetry breaking, with an operator language that may help characterize such states on quantum processors.
