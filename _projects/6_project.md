Transitions out of non-Abelian topological order are hard to describe in microscopic quantum models with numerical methods that stay tractable at large scales. We develop a sign-free path-integral framework for Kitaev quantum doubles $$\mathcal D(G)$$ in which single-link perturbations are organized by non-invertible electric and magnetic 1-form symmetries that proliferate distinct anyon species. An exact *matterization* map turns the model into a $$G$$ gauge–Higgs theory, and generalized Fredenhagen–Marcu order parameters detect which anyons condense. For $$G = S_3$$, quantum Monte Carlo shows that proliferating a non-Abelian electric anyon drives a first-order transition in which all nontrivial electric anyons condense, while the purely magnetic limit shows a first-order confinement transition, giving a unified numerical framework for anyon condensation, confinement and generalized symmetry breaking.
